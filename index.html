Two routes. One standard.

Intelligence when you are uncertain. Curation when the object is exceptional.

The site deliberately uses two psychological regimes: low-friction evidence capture for assessment and slower, more selective pacing for acquisition.

Friction is only justified when it improves evidence quality, protects trust, changes meaning or increases legitimate selectivity.

The uncertainty-first method

Useful output before perfect evidence.

Most people do not begin with laboratory photographs, complete provenance or precise measurements. They begin with a box, a tray, a certificate, a memory or a question.

Snapshot Assessment

One or a few broad photos. Get a category guess, visible highlights, obvious risk areas and the next best capture.

Low-friction curiosity conversion.

Quick Capture

Basic item photographs and notes. Build probable identification, rough bands, visible condition notes and prioritised follow-up.

Fast triage before demanding more effort.

Structured Capture

Measurements, certificate data, provenance and valuation purpose. Tighten the evidence packet and confidence architecture.

The core reusable assessment state.

Specialist Review

High-value, suspicious or conflicting cases stop rather than bluffing past the edge of evidence.

Escalation is a feature, not a failure.
