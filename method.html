Method

Manage uncertainty. Do not disguise it.

The platform separates observation, inference, missing evidence, confidence and valuation purpose so that imperfect evidence remains commercially useful without becoming false certainty.

Operating law

Any input should produce some output.

Better input should tighten the conclusion. It should not determine whether the system works at all.

Observe

Record what is directly visible or extracted from submitted evidence.

Facts first.

Infer

Build candidate interpretations without silently promoting them into facts.

Hypothesis stays labelled.

Expose gaps

Name value-sensitive detail that is not yet visible and identify contradictions.

Missing is not failure.

Guide

Rank the next action by expected uncertainty reduction or commercial payoff.

Every output directs effort.

Confidence architecture

Four confidence measures because four different things can fail.

Identification

How likely is the item correctly identified on current evidence?

Authenticity

How comfortable is the system that the item appears genuine, unaltered or correctly matched?

Condition

How reliable is the visible condition judgement given image quality and completeness?

Value

How dependable is the provisional range for the selected valuation purpose?

System orchestration

Modular prompts, typed outputs and explicit review.

Recommended event flow

UPLOAD ↓ NORMALISE ↓ COMPLETENESS / BLOCKER CHECK ↓ CATEGORY MODULE ↓ CONFIDENCE ↓ VALUATION MODE ↓ RANGE ↓ QA ↓ REPORT ↓ REGISTRY ↓ GUIDANCE / ESCALATION
P1

Intake Normaliser

Raw uploads and rough notes become a clean working packet while the originals remain preserved.

P2

Blocker Checker

Missing media, contradictions and obvious evidence gaps surface before expensive deeper processing.

P10

Adversarial QA

Assumptions, confidence and blocker handling get challenged before configured reports release.

Visual doctrine

Museum outside. Analyst workstation inside.

Public acquisition pages use space, object weight and editorial pacing. Evidence capture and reporting use denser, clearer work surfaces.

Prestige does not justify poor usability. Deliberate pacing belongs where it changes meaning. Assessment intake should remain permissive until the system has created enough value to justify asking for more.

Non-negotiable limits

Trust increases when the system states exactly where its evidence stops.

Loose coin authenticity cannot be guaranteed from ordinary consumer photographs alone. Coin grading precision and rare variety attribution may require deeper specialist review and better imaging.

A gemstone grading certificate is not a valuation. Certificate verification and physical stone-to-report consistency are separate states.

Provisional ranges are purpose-specific. Pricing evidence ages and should retain source dates and revalidation state.
