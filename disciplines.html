Disciplines

Category-specific trust. Backbone-level consistency.

The workflow remains stable across collectible types. The evidence rules, risk controls and category fields change by discipline.

Initial live lane

Numismatics

Raw loose coins.

A deliberately difficult first lane: high visual variability, missing measurements, cleaning, damage, counterfeit risk and grade sensitivity.

Minimum viable intake

One or more clear photographs, ideally front and back, plus any rough owner note.

Structured capture adds

Obverse, reverse and edge photographs, weight, diameter, thickness, ruler reference, metal if known, provenance notes and close-ups of unusual or damaged areas.

Assess a Coin

Gemmology

Certificate-backed gemstones.

A document-and-image lane designed to separate certificate extraction, issuing-source verification and physical item consistency.

Minimum viable intake

Certificate image or PDF, report number if visible, one clear stone photograph and stated use case if known.

Structured capture adds

Certificate file, report number, front and side photographs, inscription evidence, dimensions, carat weight, invoice or prior valuation, origin note and intended valuation purpose.
