# Asset notes

The website uses optimised WebP derivatives of images supplied in the August 2026 ZA Collectibles source archives.

- `za-crest.png`: supplied ZA Collectibles silver crest.
- `coin-*`: supplied coin photographs/scans from `001.zip` and `R01-ZA COLLECTIBLES_.zip`.
- `gem-*`: supplied gemstone photographs from `R01-ZA COLLECTIBLES_.zip`.
- `stamps-*`: supplied philatelic collection photographs from `R01-ZA COLLECTIBLES_.zip`.
- `sword-*`: supplied edged-weapon and research material from `R01-ZA COLLECTIBLES_.zip`.
- `pack-*`, `dossier-*`, `museum-black.webp`, `caracalla-card.webp`: supplied presentation and research examples from `COINS + NOTES.zip`.
