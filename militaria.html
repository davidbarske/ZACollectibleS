Material history

A Named Medal Group is Not Merely Metal and Ribbon.

The evidence may sit across an engraving, service record, photograph, uniform detail or family letter. Separating those elements can destroy the very context that makes the group understandable.

  • Markings Recipient, regiment, maker, inspection and issue marks.
  • Construction Materials, fittings, wear and period consistency.
  • Alteration Replacement parts, refinishing and later assembly.
  • Provenance Family history, documents, photographs and sale history.
