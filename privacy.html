Privacy & Data Handling

How information submitted through the ZA Collectibles website is intended to be handled.

Contact us →

Information You Provide

Enquiries may contain contact details, descriptions of objects, photographs, certificates, provenance notes and other supporting material. Only provide information you are entitled to share.

Online Enquiry Forms

The current GitHub Pages version prepares enquiry information in the visitor’s browser. Photographs selected in the form are previewed locally and are not transmitted by this static version. A server-side delivery service can be connected without changing the public form design.

When Online Delivery is Enabled

The live deployment should identify the service provider, retention rules, access controls and deletion process that apply to submitted information.

Contact

Questions about data handling can be raised through the contact page.
