Inspection clues

A Convincing Silhouette is Not Enough.

Originals, later assemblies, restorations and high-quality replicas can look persuasive from a distance. Close inspection asks whether construction, materials, marks, wear and finish are consistent with the claimed period and pattern.

  • Blade Profile, fuller, ricasso, spine, temper, polish and markings.
  • Hilt Guard, grip, wire, pommel, peening and later replacement.
  • Scabbard Fit, throat, mounts, finish, repairs and period consistency.
  • Provenance Ownership, service history, labels, documents and past sales.
Research example

Original, Restored or Replica?

A useful review separates direct observation from inference. It records each component, compares manufacture and markings against reference material, identifies replaced or restored parts and states clearly where specialist physical examination is still required.

  1. 01 Photograph full length and component details
  2. 02 Compare pattern, marks and construction
  3. 03 Document conclusions and unresolved questions
Useful first record

Photograph the Details Before Cleaning or Disassembly.

Send a full view of both sides, blade and spine marks, hilt from several angles, scabbard and fittings, overall length, blade length and any documents or labels. Do not polish, sharpen or force components apart for photographs.
