A Fold, Signature or Serial Can Change the Reading.

Banknotes reward patient inspection. The same basic design may exist across printers, signatures, prefixes and replacement issues, while condition can be affected by pressing, washing, repair and trimming.

  • Issue Issuer, date, denomination, series and signatures.
  • Manufacture Paper, watermark, printer and security features.
  • Condition Folds, pinholes, stains, tears, repairs and pressing.
  • Rarity Prefix, serial type, replacement note and market depth.
Collection example

Sort by Issue Before Sorting by Price.

An inherited currency group may mix circulation notes, commemorative issues, foreign travel remnants and stronger collector material. A simple inventory by country, issuer, series and condition makes the hidden structure visible and prevents a key note disappearing into a bulk lot.
