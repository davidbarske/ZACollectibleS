Small evidence, close inspection

Front, Back, Margin and Cancellation All Carry Information.

Catalogue identity is only one layer. Perforations, watermark, gum, hinges, thins, repairs, centring and postal use can separate two apparently similar examples.

  • Issue Country, date, printing, shade and watermark.
  • Condition Gum, hinges, tears, thinning, foxing and repairs.
  • Postal history Route, date, rate, cover and cancellation.
  • Collection logic Completeness, duplication, gaps and past notes.

From Inherited Album to a Collection Someone Can Understand.

The aim is not to make every page decorative. It is to retain the collector’s logic, identify the stronger material and give the next owner a clear route through the story. A black-book presentation can combine the stamp, issue information, postal context and condition notes without turning the collection into a catalogue dump.
