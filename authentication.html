Research & authentication

Trust Begins with
What the Evidence Can Support.

Research, provenance and authenticity review are handled as evidence problems, with conclusions kept separate from what remains unresolved.

Request a review → Contact us →

Object Evidence

Marks, construction, measurements, surfaces, inscriptions, materials and condition-sensitive details.

Document Evidence

Certificates, invoices, provenance notes, labels, old photographs and prior research.

Context Evidence

Period consistency, known comparables, collection history and specialist reference material.

Authentication discipline

Confidence is Not a Certificate-shaped Word.

A document can support an assessment without proving the physical object matches it. A plausible object can still need deeper testing. The useful question is not whether a screen can pronounce something “genuine”, but what evidence supports that conclusion and what could still overturn it.

Escalate When the Evidence Demands It

High-value, suspicious, contradictory or technically specialised objects may require physical examination, testing or referral to a category specialist. Escalation is a trust control, not a failure of the process.

Preserve Weak Clues

Informal notes and family history should be retained and labelled for what they are rather than discarded or silently upgraded into fact.
