Collection services

Build, Document and Preserve
A Collection with Intention.

Collection work can involve much more than buying objects: structure, research, gaps, documentation, presentation and succession all matter.

Discuss a collection → See presentation work →
01

Collection Mapping

Build an inventory, identify themes, duplicates, strengths, gaps and evidence needs.

02

Gap Analysis

Define what would materially improve or complete the collection rather than simply adding volume.

03

Research & Provenance

Turn scattered notes, certificates and memories into a durable record that remains attached to the objects.

04

Acquisition Support

Source missing items, screen opportunities and help compare candidates before purchase.

05

Presentation Systems

Cards, booklets, catalogues, cases and consistent visual systems for the collection as a whole.

06

Disposition Planning

Plan retention, gifting, sale, auction, insurance or estate handling with the collection understood as a system.

A collection is more than the sum of its objects

The Structure Becomes Part of the Value.

A coherent collection tells a stronger story, is easier to manage and can be easier for the next person to understand. Documentation and presentation are therefore not merely decorative.

A South African heritage and collectibles house helping people understand, acquire, sell, preserve and present objects with history.
