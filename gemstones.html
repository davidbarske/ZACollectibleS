A Certificate is Useful Only When the Item Agrees with It.

Report number, measurements, weight, inscription, colour description, clarity characteristics and disclosed treatments should form a consistent picture. A discrepancy is a reason to slow down, not to force a conclusion.

  • Identity Material, variety, cut and stated origin.
  • Consistency Measurements, carat weight and inscription.
  • Treatment Heating, filling, coating and other disclosures.
  • Purpose Insurance replacement is not the same as resale value.

Three Views Can Answer Three Different Questions.

A face-up image shows colour and cut. A side view shows profile, setting and possible wear. Magnified detail can reveal inclusions, abrasions and treatment clues. Combined with a report, those views create a useful starting record for specialist review.
