Completeness Is Condition

The Box, Instructions and One Tiny Missing Part Can Change Everything.

Modern collectibles punish generic assessment. A set number, production run, regional box, sticker sheet, minifigure variant or replacement component can materially change desirability. We evaluate the object as a complete collecting proposition, not merely as a toy.

LEGO & Construction Sets

Build the Evidence Around the Build.

  • Set and production variant
  • Completeness and correct minifigures
  • Original box, manuals and inserts
  • Discolouration, substitutions and wear
  • Retired status and collector demand
